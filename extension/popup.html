what the extension did

why it works this way

Job boards explicitly forbid scripted submissions and enforce it with captchas + fingerprinting, and any server-side “auto apply” would need your password. So this extension automates the typing inside your own session and stops before sending. Same net time saving, no banned account, no leaked credentials.

selector coverage

Generic label/name/placeholder heuristics cover most Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workable forms. Workday and iCIMS use obfuscated ids — “Detect fields” shows what we mapped; anything unmapped you fill by hand, and you can pin it with data-qa-field if you maintain a custom board.
